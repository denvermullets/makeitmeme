# Finished Games are permanent snapshots

Recaps are permanent, but Players are deleted when they Leave, when they're kicked, or when their Room expires, and the Admin can edit or delete Templates. So a Game keeps its own copy of everything its Recap shows. Each Game has one game-player row per Player dealt in, which holds a copy of the nickname, the Buddies left and the total. Hands, Memes and Votes point at that row, not at the Player, and its link to the Player becomes null when the Player goes. A Meme is a baked PNG and stores its caption texts and cached points and Vote counts. Its `template_id` becomes null when the Template is hard-deleted. An expired Room is marked `expired_at` and its row is kept, so Games keep a valid `room_id`, while its Players and deal history are deleted. Join codes are unique only among live Rooms. The alternative was to keep Players and Templates around (soft delete) so the Recap could join through them. We rejected it because nicknames aren't identities across Rooms and a deleted Template must actually disappear from the Admin's library.
